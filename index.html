Research interests

I am interested in how robots perceive and act in the physical world. My focus areas are:

  • Visual perception for robotics: detection and scene understanding from camera data (for example, my lane-marking detection work with OpenCV and Keras).
  • Machine learning for control and decision-making: learning-based methods for autonomous systems.
  • Safe evaluation of robot policies: simulation-based testing and what it can and cannot certify for real-world robots.
  • Deployable ML systems: turning models into reliable, scalable services.
